Listener mail metadata only

When enabled, newly received mail from outside your domains is sent to Listener with its date, sender, recipients, attachment presence and attachment names. Subjects, message bodies and attachment contents are never queued or sent.

Accounts to monitor

HTTPS is recommended for domain-network access. The receiver is a separate Listener Mail Metadata Receiver service on port 6517, not the TCP collector port 6515 or dashboard port 5080.
